- One account can own at most 3 workspaces; the fourth is refused with 403 "You can own up to 3 workspaces, so delete one before creating another." and writes no row. Memberships in other people's workspaces do not count, because joining a colleague's workspace is not the account creating its own. An invitation is refused with 403 "This workspace has no free seat, so cancel a pending invitation or add seats." when members plus live pending invitations already reach the workspace's paid seats; a refusal writes no invitation row and sends no email. The same sentence answers a re-send of an invitation the workspace has no seat for, and that re-send leaves the row and its expiry as they were. A cancelled or expired invitation frees its seat. Invitations that were pending before this change stay valid and count. One seat, the owner's, before a workspace subscribes. Limits on workspaces and invitations are held in the database by three triggers in `migrations/0004_workspace_limits.sql`, so two requests arriving at the same moment cannot both pass.
