- A column whose name says it counts what was sold, such as `NetSalesQty`, is no longer guessed as Sales just because the name holds `net`; Sales falls to another money column or stays empty. A column whose name says a price, such as `net_unit_price`, `net_price`, `total_price` or `avg_price`, is never guessed as Sales either, while a name that only says money with `net`, such as `net_sales`, `Net Revenue` or `Nettoumsatz`, keeps its guess.
