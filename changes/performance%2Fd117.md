- A saved setup now has bounds: at most as many levels as the file has columns and never more than 1,000, no column in two levels, level names of at most 300 characters unless the name is one of that level's own columns, and at most as many filters as columns, each with 500 values of at most 300 characters. A setup with 1,000 columns of 300 characters each is still accepted.
- A setup or bridge save over 6 MiB is refused with `This is too large to save.` before the body is parsed, whether or not the request states its size.
