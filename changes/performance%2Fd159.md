- A price change that displays as exactly a band edge now counts in the band its label names: +10.0% reads under "Up 5% to 10%" and -10.0% under "Down 5% to 10%", and the same holds at 0.5%, 2% and 5%. The Price changes tab compares the change as a percentage rounded to six decimals, so the division's floating-point error no longer pushes an exact edge into the next band. A change that is not an edge stays where it was: at two-decimal prices up to 10,000 the nearest distinct change to an edge is 0.0001 of a percent.
- `src/engine.ts`: `PRICE_BUCKETS` holds percentage edges, and `priceDistribution` compares the rounded percentage with each edge belonging to the band nearer no change. The labels, the bands and every other engine result are unchanged.
- `test/engine.test.ts`: one test covers every edge at the edge and one cent either side. One money tie-out test holds a four- and five-decimal fixture whose whole dollars change if its inputs are rounded to three decimals: the file holds 20.49999 and 21.49998 and shows 20 and 21, while three-decimal inputs hold 20.500 and 21.500 and show 21 and 22.
