- A price change that is exactly a band edge now counts in the band its label names: +10.0% reads under "Up 5% to 10%" and -10.0% under "Down 5% to 10%", and the same holds at 0.5%, 2% and 5%. The Price changes tab decides such a change from the configuration's exact decimal sales and units, so the division's floating-point error cannot push an exact edge into the next band, and a change that is not exactly an edge keeps its own band even when it is only 1e-10 of a percent past one, at any price.
- `src/engine.ts`: `PRICE_BUCKETS` holds percentage edges, and `priceDistribution` decides a change near an edge on the exact decimal sales and units, comparing whole numbers instead of dividing, with an edge belonging to the band nearer no change. The labels, the bands and every other engine result are unchanged.
- `test/engine.test.ts`: one test covers every edge at the edge and one cent either side. One test holds seven changes that are just past a bound, four at four-decimal prices and three at 100,000,000, and each stays in its own band. One money tie-out test holds a four- and five-decimal fixture whose whole dollars change if its inputs are rounded to three decimals: the file holds 20.49999 and 21.49998 and shows 20 and 21, while three-decimal inputs hold 20.500 and 21.500 and show 21 and 22.
