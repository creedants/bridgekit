- German column names now guess their measures: Menge, Stück, Anzahl and Absatz guess units, and Umsatz and Erlöse guess sales. A German unit price such as Stückpreis or Mengenpreis stays out of units, and a tax column such as Umsatzsteuer stays out of sales. `src/app/setup.ts` folds umlauts after NFC normalization, so Stück and Stueck match the same stems, and bare Stück and Erlöse still guess.
- The whole-row separator note moved out of the Data check sentences into the tab warnings: `decimalCommaNote` in `src/app/numbers.ts` takes an optional whole-row flag that defaults to on, `dataCheckNotes` in `src/app/Results.tsx` calls it with the flag off, and a `useWholeRowWarning` hook loads the note with a stale guard. `checkWarnings` appends the loaded note, so the Data check tab count includes it.
