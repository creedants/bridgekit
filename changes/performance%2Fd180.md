A Windows-1252 CSV whose first non-ASCII byte is past 64 KiB can now be retried as text. An unmarked UTF-8 CSV cut inside its final character loads at or under 32 MiB with its incomplete tail dropped, and a database out-of-memory failure is reported as too big without the encoding retry. A file over 32 MiB that needs the whole-file retry keeps the old refusal.
