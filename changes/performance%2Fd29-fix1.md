- The workspace and invitation limits move from the hooks alone into the database, through three triggers in `migrations/0004_workspace_limits.sql`. Refusing an owner membership past the third owned workspace now deletes the workspace the same request wrote, so a create that races another leaves one workspace rather than none or two. Refusing an invitation without a seat deletes its row, and refusing a re-send puts the expiry back, so the row stays as it was. The refusal sentences are unchanged. Nothing else changes: no existing row is read, changed or deleted, and the migration applies to a database that already holds workspaces over the limits.
