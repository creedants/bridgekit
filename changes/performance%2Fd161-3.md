Deleting an account now asks Paddle to cancel a live subscription before the workspace and its subscription row go. Before, the delete removed only rows, and the `subscription` row cascaded away with the workspace while Paddle kept billing a subscription nobody could reach.

- `server/paddle.ts` reads the subscription before it cancels. `cancelSubscription` first gets `/subscriptions/{id}`, then posts `/subscriptions/{id}/cancel`, and the cancel counts only when the answer parses as JSON, names the subscription that was asked for, and reports either `canceled` or `scheduled_change.action` of `cancel`. Anything else, including a body that is not JSON or holds no subscription entity, throws `PaddleCancelError` and the deletion deletes nothing.
- A cancel Paddle already holds is settled and gets no second request, whether it came from an earlier attempt or was scheduled in the Paddle portal. The state is read from Paddle every time rather than from the stored row, so it survives `subscription.updated` reconciliation: a scheduled cancel leaves Paddle's status `active`, and `syncSubscription` in `server/workspace.ts` writes that back over the row. Without the read, a retry would send a second cancel, which Paddle refuses as `subscription_locked_pending_changes` while the first is pending.
- A `paused` subscription is cancelled immediately, because Paddle refuses a scheduled cancellation for one. `active`, `trialing` and `past_due` keep `effective_from: "next_billing_period"`, which is the period `hasPro` and `assertCheckoutOpen` grant Pro through.
- `server/workspace.ts` still skips a row already stored as `canceled` and marks the row `canceled` once Paddle accepts, then deletes the organizations only after every sole workspace is settled. A Paddle failure refuses the whole deletion with `SUBSCRIPTION_CANCEL_SENTENCE`, thrown as an `APIError` so better-call answers with that sentence and a 503.
- `server/auth.ts` passes `paddleAccount(env)` to `deleteSoleWorkspaces`, so deleting an account through the Worker cancels each sole workspace's live subscription before any row goes. `paddleAccount` and `seatPrices` live in `server/paddle.ts`, so the auth module reads them without importing the Worker.
- `test/paddle.test.ts` covers the read before the cancel, `effective_from` for `active`, `trialing`, `past_due` and `paused`, and refusal of a non-JSON answer, an empty entity, a live subscription reported with no cancel, and an answer naming another subscription. `test/workspace.test.ts` updates the Paddle fakes to answer the read and the cancel, and keeps the retry test where a refused cancel leaves the other workspace alone. `test/worker-routes.test.ts` drives the real `POST /api/auth/delete-user` request with a fake Paddle: a cancel answer that is not JSON or holds no subscription entity keeps every row, a subscription cancelled in the portal takes no cancel request, and the review's sequence of a partial cancel, reconciliation back to `active`, then a successful retry sends exactly one cancel per subscription.
- `privacy.html` says a workspace with a paid subscription is deleted only after its subscription is cancelled in Paddle. That sentence and the refusal sentence are unchanged.
