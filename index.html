Price-volume-mix analysis for products built from options

Group by model, and a shift toward a higher-priced finish looks like a price rise. Group by full configuration, and new configurations have no prior price to compare.

PVM Bridge compares each configuration with itself, and falls back to the nearest group that existed, in the order you set.
