Price-volume-mix (PVM) analysis

See why sales moved.

Price, volume, and a mix step for every product attribute, in one bridge.

For finance, FP&A and sales operations.

A bridge is a chart that starts at last period's sales, adds or subtracts each cause, and ends at this period's sales.

How it works

  1. Load a CSV or Excel export.

    It is read in your browser, not uploaded.

  2. Put your attributes in order.

    Most important first. Each mix step is measured after the ones above it.

  3. Read the bridge, then the rows behind it.

    Select a step to open the Movers table.

Questions

Which files does it read?
CSV and Excel (.xlsx, first sheet), or cells pasted from Excel with their header row.
How large can a file be?
No row limit; the sample has 1,000,000 rows. Your browser's memory is the ceiling.
Do the steps add up?
Yes, for every configuration, so the bridge reconciles in total and for any subtotal.
Does it export to Excel?
Export Excel writes the bridge, coverage, movers and, for up to 150,000 configurations, a detail sheet with live formulas.
Who runs it?
Marcus Gibson, an individual in the United States.
