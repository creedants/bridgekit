Privacy

The short version: your sales file never leaves your computer, and none of its data is kept once the tab is closed unless you save bridge totals to a Pro workspace. The tool remembers your column choices in your browser, and you can turn that off.

Your files

  • The tool reads your file inside the browser tab. It is not uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • The calculation runs in your browser, using a database engine that is downloaded as part of the page.
  • Exports (Excel, CSV, PNG and the HTML report) and the setup file are created in your browser and saved by your browser.

What is stored, and where

  • Your file and its rows are never stored. They live in the tab’s memory and is gone when you close or reload the tab, or choose Clear data.
  • Your setup is remembered unless you turn it off. After you calculate a bridge, your column choices, attribute order and options are saved in your browser’s local storage, so the next file with the same columns opens ready. This includes your column names and attribute names, but no values from your data: filter selections are not saved for that reason.
  • Unticking Remember this setup in this browser deletes everything the tool saved. The tool then keeps one setting, that remembering is off, so it stays off next time.
  • Local storage stays on your computer; it is not sent to us. Clearing your browser’s site data also removes it.
  • The tool does not use IndexedDB, browser caches, a private file system or a service worker.
  • The site sets no cookies and has no accounts.
  • The site uses cookies for sign-in. Microsoft and Google sign-in use a short-lived cookie. A session cookie keeps you signed in; signing out removes it.
  • If you write to support@pvmbridge.com, your message and email address are kept only to answer you.

Optional AI commentary

  • Below the summary there is a button to have Anthropic’s Claude write a longer commentary. Nothing is sent unless you press it, each time.
  • What is sent: the bridge totals, the names of the measure, attributes and bridge lines, the two period labels and the match coverage. You can see the exact text under Show exactly what is sent before you press the button.
  • What is not sent: rows, your file, and any value from your data such as customer, product or region names.
  • The totals go to our server, which adds our API key and passes them to Anthropic. Our server does not store them. Anthropic handles them under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train models on them.

Pro workspaces (optional)

Nobody needs an account to use the tool. If you sign in to a Pro workspace:

  • What is stored: your email address (and your name and the sign-in tokens that Microsoft or Google return, if you sign in with them), the workspaces you belong to, their members and invitations, and what members choose to save: setups and bridge totals. A saved setup holds column names, attribute order and options. A saved bridge holds the totals you see in the bridge table, the attribute names and the names of any filtered columns.
  • What is not stored: your file, its rows, filter selections or any other value from your data. Sign-in sessions do not record your IP address or browser. Your IP address is used briefly to slow repeated sign-in attempts, and PVM Bridge does not store it.
  • Who sees it: members of the workspace. Any member can delete a saved item.
  • Companies involved: Cloudflare hosts the site and the database. Resend sends sign-in and invitation emails. Paddle takes payment as our reseller, on its own checkout page, and holds your billing details; we receive the subscription id, the customer id, the price id, the status, the seat count and the end date of the paid period. If you sign in with Microsoft or Google, they confirm who you are.
  • Deleting: Delete account in the tool deletes your account. Workspaces where you are the only member are deleted with everything saved in them.

What we can see

  • The site has no analytics or tracking scripts. The tool loads nothing from third parties.
  • The site has no tracking scripts. The tool loads nothing from third parties.
  • The site’s server counts page loads: for each page, the date and the website you came from, if any. It does not record your IP address, set a cookie or keep anything else about you. In the tool, it counts only that the page was opened.
  • The checkout page at /pay/ loads Paddle’s script to take payment.
  • The company hosting the site can see ordinary web server logs, such as the pages requested and IP addresses. It cannot see your files.

Checking this yourself

Open your browser’s developer tools, go to the network panel, and load a file. No request carries your data. The pages also carry a content security policy that allows connections only to this site, so the browser itself blocks anything else. The checkout page allows connections to Paddle, which takes the payment. The source code is published so anyone can confirm what the page does, and its automated test checks that none of your data is stored or sent.

If this changes

Planned paid features such as saved workspaces across devices would store your setup on a server. This page will be updated before any such feature is released, and uploading data will never be required to use the free tool.
